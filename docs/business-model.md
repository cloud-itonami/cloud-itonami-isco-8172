# Business Model: Independent Wood Processing Operations

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-8172`
- ISCO-08: `8172`
- Occupation: Wood Processing Plant Operators

## Customer

- small sawmills
- furniture makers
- lumber processors

## Offer

- process monitoring
- safety and dust-level sampling
- batch reporting
- compliance tracking

## Revenue

- monthly plant-operations platform fee
- per-batch fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no robot dispatch near cutting blades without governor gate
- blade-change/maintenance procedures require human sign-off
- safety-envelope data is auditable, not editable
